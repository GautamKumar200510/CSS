Iron Man

Iron Man is a fictional character and superhero from Marvel Comics. He was created by writer and editor Stan Lee, developed by scripter Larry Lieber, and designed by artists Don Heck and Jack Kirby. Iron Man made his first appearance in Tales of Suspense.

Publication History

Iron Man's publication history includes his debut in Tales of Suspense #39 (1963) and subsequent appearances in various Marvel publications.

Characteristics of Biography

Iron Man is known for his intelligence, wealth, and technological prowess. He is often depicted as a genius inventor and engineer who uses his advanced suit of armor to fight crime and protect the world.
